MICROSCOPIC: The predominant component of the tumor is an infiltrating ductal
earemma with almost no tubule formation. Extensive necrosis is seen in the
trating component. A minor component of comedo type intraductal carcinoma is seen
at the periphery, making up less than quarter of the tumor. Scattered through the tumor
are multiple foci of sarcomatous metaplasia characterized by myxoid stroma of high
exilularity with rudimentary cartilaginous differentiation. Although small foci of this
metaplasia are scattered through the tumor, the epithelial component of the tumor
predominates. Tumor extends to the margin of resection. The adjacent breast shows duet
ertusic and papillary apocrine metaplasia.
