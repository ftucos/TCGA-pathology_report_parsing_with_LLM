COMMENT:
Representative sections of invasive urothelial carcinoma are submitted for p53
assay by immunohistology, results of which will be issued in an addendum.
There are changes in the prostatic adenocarcinoma that suggest prior therapy
(radiation, hormone or both). And while the bulk of the carcinoma exhibits either
fused or separate but distinct malignant glands, there is a small component of
tumor characterized by individual infiltrating malignant cells. Finally, tumor is
focally seen in and around skeletal muscle fibers in the proximal anterior
prostate, with no associated benign glandular tissue seen in the same area.
Although the capsule in this area is poorly defined or virtually nonexistent, the
changes are interpreted as representing prostatic adenocarcinoma outside the
prostate.
